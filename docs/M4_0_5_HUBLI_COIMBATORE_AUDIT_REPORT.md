## Executive Summary & Root Cause Verdict

### Core Question
> *Is Bengaluru an intentional intermediate transfer or an unintentional/stale coordinate substitution in the Hubli → Coimbatore journey?*

### Definitive Finding: NEITHER
1. **Zero Bengaluru Coordinates in the Journey Data:**
   Across all 5 generated journeys for Hubli → Coimbatore (both backend orchestrator and client fallback), there is **zero presence of Bengaluru**. The origin is strictly `Hubli City Hub` (`15.3497°N, 75.1090°E`), the destination is strictly `Coimbatore City Hub` (`11.0318°N, 76.9708°E`), and the intermediate transfer stations are strictly `Hubli Railway Station` (`15.3697°N, 75.1290°E`) and `Coimbatore Railway Station` (`11.0218°N, 76.9608°E`).
2. **Closest Point on Route to Bengaluru:**
   The closest point on the entire route geometry to Bengaluru (`12.9716°N, 77.5946°E`) is at `(12.53623°N, 76.50479°E)` (Waypoint 8 of Segment 1), located **127.72 km west-southwest of Bengaluru**. No segment vertex, waypoint, or marker is within 50 km or 100 km of Bengaluru.

### Why the User Observed the Defect
The user reported:
> *"The header displays Hubli → Coimbatore, but the visible route geometry appears to connect the Hubli region towards Bengaluru. The final Coimbatore endpoint is not visible in the screenshot."*

This visual phenomenon is caused by a compound intersection of:
1. **Corridor Trajectory & Compass Bearing (The "Optical Illusion"):**
   - The great-circle bearing from Hubli (`15.35°N, 75.11°E`) to Coimbatore (`11.03°N, 76.97°E`) is **157.01° (South-Southeast)**.
   - For comparison, the bearing from Hubli to Bengaluru (`12.97°N, 77.59°E`) is **138.54° (Southeast)**.
   - The rail corridor (`deterministic_train_corridor`) generated by `generate_curved_corridor` travels south through Central Karnataka: Hubli (`15.37°N`) → Davanagere (`14.34°N`) → Arsikere (`13.27°N`) → Hassan / Channarayapatna (`12.90°N`) → Mysore (`12.16°N`) → Coimbatore (`11.02°N`).
   - At Waypoint 7 (`12.9047°N, 76.3666°E`), the route passes at the **exact latitude of Bengaluru** (`12.97°N`). On an unlabelled or uncluttered overview map of Southern India, an observer seeing a line traveling southeast down from Hubli into Southern Karnataka naturally assumes it is pointing towards Bengaluru.
2. **Viewport Aspect Ratio Mismatch & Bottom Clipping:**
   - The Hubli → Coimbatore journey is a **heavily elongated vertical corridor**:
     - Latitude span: **4.3479° (~485 km North-to-South)**.
     - Longitude span: **1.8618° (~201 km East-to-West)**.
     - Spatial aspect ratio: **2.4 : 1 (Height : Width)**.
   - The Leaflet map container in `JourneyMap.tsx` has fixed CSS height: `h-80 sm:h-96 w-full` (384px height on desktop, 320px on mobile), spanning up to 1200px wide (aspect ratio **1 : 3.125**).
   - Because Leaflet's `fitBounds` must constrain by the vertical dimension, at desktop size (384px height - 90px padding = 294px usable height), the vertical zoom ceiling is `zoomY = 6.53`. Because Leaflet integer zooming rounds down, it selects zoom level 6.
3. **Map Initialization & Layout Invalidation Lifecycle Gap:**
   - In `ResultsPage.tsx`, the map component mounts while the TanStack Query for `enrichedRoute` is pending (`isLoading`). The initial render mounts with client fallback route bounds.
   - When `enrichedRoute` resolves asynchronously (100–300ms later), the Leaflet layers effect executes `map.fitBounds(boundsObj, { padding: [45, 45], maxZoom: 14, animate: true })`.
   - However, `map.invalidateSize()` is **only** called in the initial `viewMode` effect with a 150ms timeout. If the container dimensions or flex layout settle asynchronously, or if an animation is in flight, Leaflet fits bounds against a stale or partial container viewport, centering the view higher up (towards Central Karnataka) and **visually clipping the Coimbatore destination marker below the bottom edge of the viewport**.
4. **UI Overlay Occlusion:**
   - The bottom-left of the map is occluded by the Route Legend indicator (`absolute bottom-2 left-2 z-[1000]`), and the bottom-right is occluded by the OSM/CARTO attribution badge (`absolute bottom-1 right-2 z-[1000]`).
   - Because Coimbatore (`11.0218°N, 76.9608°E`) lands at the very bottom-center/bottom-right margin of the fitted bounds, these overlays further obscure or crop the red Destination marker.

## Phase 4 — Inspect Map Rendering (`JourneyMap.tsx`)

1. **Geometry Priority Order:**
   - Lines 225–255: `effectiveRoute` prioritizes `enrichedRoute` from `/api/v1/maps/enrich`. If unavailable or diverged > 45 km, it falls back to `buildClientFallbackRoute(targetJourney)`.
   - Lines 441–456: In segment polyline construction:
     (1) `enrichedSeg.geojson_geometry.coordinates`
     (2) `seg.geojsonGeometry.coordinates`
     (3) `enrichedSeg.coordinates`
     (4) Endpoint-to-endpoint straight line fallback.
2. **Polyline Rendering:**
   - Segments are rendered individually with mode-specific styles and active casing halos (Rapido `#7c3aed`, Train `#9333ea`, Cab `#2563eb`).
   - Compliant with RFC 7946: coordinate pairs `[longitude, latitude]` are inverted to Leaflet's `[latitude, longitude]`.
   - Only segments with $\ge 2$ valid coordinates are rendered; invalid or Null Island points are strictly stripped.
3. **Marker Rendering:**
   - Waypoints are filtered for `isValidCoordinate`.
   - Distinct markers: Green pulsing marker for Origin, Amber marker for Transfer Hubs (with cyan halo if XR scene exists), and Red marker for Destination.
4. **`map.fitBounds` Behavior & Invalidation Timing:**
   - Lines 732–746: `map.fitBounds(boundsObj, { padding: [45, 45], maxZoom: 14, animate: true })` calculates bounds over all valid polyline points and waypoint markers.
   - **Timing Defect Identified:** `map.invalidateSize()` is only called in the initial `viewMode` effect with a 150ms timeout. When `enrichedRoute` updates asynchronously after TanStack Query resolves, `fitBounds` executes without verifying whether the container has settled its rendered height.
5. **Alternative Journeys:**
   - Rendered as dashed gray lines (`#94a3b8`, opacity 0.55). All connect Hubli to Coimbatore; none introduce Bengaluru geometry.
6. **Hardcoded Coordinate Audit:**
   - Full grep across the codebase verified that Bengaluru coordinates (`12.9716, 77.5946`) exist only in the Bangalore gazetteer entry and explicit Bangalore unit test cases. No fallback or default coordinates inject Bengaluru when `origin=Hubli&destination=Coimbatore`.
